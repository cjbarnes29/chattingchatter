What Chatter does today

Everything listed here is in the app you can download now.

Private by design

Controls that decide who sees what on your phone.

PIN lock and decoy PIN

Chatter asks for your PIN on opening and when you log out. A second, decoy PIN opens a different set of chats. Free accounts get 1 decoy chat.

Locked messages

Lock individual messages behind your fingerprint, face or phone passcode, on phones that support it.

Protected account changes

Changing your password needs a code sent to your email. Changing your email needs the answer to your security question.

Online status you control

Show as online, offline or automatic. Turn read receipts off if you prefer.

Talk your way

The everyday messenger parts.

Chats that feel alive

Delivery and read ticks, live typing, swipe to reply, reactions, message tags and Timeline Jump.

Groups, stories and contacts

Group chats, stories that expire, and contacts you can favourite, archive or block.

Voice and video calls

Call a friend or start a call in a group chat. Calls need microphone and camera permission and are still in beta.

Shared events

Add an event with an alarm in a one-to-one chat. It is posted into the conversation.

Quick replies and dictation

Suggested replies, plus voice typing where your phone or browser supports speech recognition.

Make it yours

Under Settings → Appearance and Profile.

Appearance

Text size, font style, tab and navigation colours, bubble colours and shape, wallpapers and animations.

Profiles

Nickname, age and status. Tap a photo to zoom it, tap a name to open the full profile.

Ghost Canvas

Tap the ghost button in a one-to-one chat to draw, drop notes, make emoji collages and play tic-tac-toe together. Ink fades away on its own.

Questions

Is Chatter free?

Yes. Chatter Pro is optional. You can start the 7-day trial from inside the app. Payments are not connected yet, so nothing is charged. Planned prices are $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year.

Are my messages end-to-end encrypted?

No. Messages are stored on Chatter’s servers and protected by account rules, so only you and the person you message can read them. Only Pro canvas keepsakes are encrypted, on your device, with your own password.

How does fingerprint unlock work?

It is a Pro setting under Privacy & security and is off by default. Set a real PIN and a decoy PIN first. Then swipe up on the lock screen. A fingerprint or face your phone accepts opens your real chats. One it rejects opens your decoy chats. Android reports a cancelled prompt the same way as a wrong fingerprint, so cancelling also opens the decoy chats.

Does it work offline?

The app opens offline, but sending and receiving messages needs an internet connection.

How do I update?

The web version shows an Update button when a new version is ready. On Android, download the newest APK from this page and install it over the old one.

Which devices are supported?

Android through the APK download, and any modern browser. On a computer you can install the web version from Chrome or Edge.
